STAY ALERT

Know the signs of a suspicious job offer.

A professional-looking advertisement is not proof that an employer or opportunity is genuine. Take time to verify the details.

01

Requests for upfront payments

Be cautious if you are asked to pay recruitment, registration, or processing fees to secure a job.

02

Unrealistic salary promises

Investigate offers promising unusually high pay for little work or experience.

03

Requests for sensitive information

Verify the employer before sharing identity documents, financial details, or other sensitive information.

04

Pressure to act immediately

Be careful when an advert pressures you to pay, share information, or accept an offer without proper verification.
